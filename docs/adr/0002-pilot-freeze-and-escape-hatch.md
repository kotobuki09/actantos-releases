# Pilot window freezes platform work except one escape hatch

Status: accepted

During the design-partner window, default work is limited to pilot-blocking fixes, docs, and policy templates on the free surface. Firecracker, multi-tenant SaaS, credential broker, SCIM, S3 WORM productization, and new framework adapters stay frozen. OIDC, gVisor, HA, and extra SDKs stay frozen unless a written Escape Hatch applies: two independent partners hit the same blocker, or one partner is strategically existential and workarounds fail. Soft freezes and parallel Phase-2 platform tracks were rejected because they dissolve focus before unaided pilot proof exists.
